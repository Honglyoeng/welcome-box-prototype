What is the welcome box?

The problem

A stay on the cardiology ward often starts suddenly. Patients are worried, tired and get a lot of information at once.

  • They don't know what will happen. Nobody tells them the plan for the day.
  • They don't want to bother the nurses. The team is busy, so questions are kept inside or forgotten.
  • Simple things get missed. How to order food, the wifi, what to bring.
  • The heart is hard to understand. Medical words are confusing without a picture.
  • They don't know who is who. Many faces, few names.
  • The days feel long and lonely. There is little to do, and family is not always there.

The idea

A small gift waiting in the room for every patient on the cardiology ward. Everything people usually ask about, in one box.

Readat your own pace
Askwith better questions
Feelat home and less alone

What is inside

  • Welcome book. Who you will meet, what a day looks like, space for your own questions and stories from other patients.
  • Food card. How meals work on the ward.
  • Things you can ask for. Small comforts you only have to ask the team for.
  • Heart and blood circulation. A simple picture of the heart and blood flow that doctors and patients can point at together, to talk things through and understand each other.
  • Exercise leaflet. Gentle movements to do step by step.
  • Pen and tote bag. To write your questions down and take everything home.

Why it matters

Calmer patientsThey know what to expect from day one.

Fewer calls for small thingsThe answers are already in the box, so the team has more time for care.

No extra work for nursesIt is ready in the room when the patient arrives.
